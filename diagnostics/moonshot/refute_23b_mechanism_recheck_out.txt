R9 -- which AXIS does each corpus scale? along-bone (changes segment length, moves the
      child joint) vs perpendicular (changes girth, moves no joint)
====================================================================================================
  For every joint j with a child c: unit bone direction u = (J_c - J_j)/|.| in rest space.
  s = exp(lbs_j - lbs_root) is the DIAGONAL that actually acts (M_j = S_root^-1 S_j).
  along     = |(s-1) component projected onto u|   -> length change of the child bone
  perp      = |(s-1) component orthogonal to u|    -> girth change only
  Averaged over all (specimen, parent-of-a-child joint).

  arm               |s-1| total   along-bone       perp  along frac
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  LIM_0                  0.6246       0.3885     0.6257       0.383
  LIM_1x                 0.6491       0.3820     0.6043       0.387
  LIM_3x                 0.6487       0.3825     0.6043       0.388
  BPX_noprior            0.6208       0.3890     0.6421       0.377
  ALL_ANTS_CLEAN         0.4208       0.2108     0.3558       0.372

  -> if the along-bone column tracks the joint-displacement column, the 3x is about
     WHERE the scale points, not only about how large it is.
